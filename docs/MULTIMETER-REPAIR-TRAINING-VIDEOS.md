## Safety and quality boundaries
1. For now use this hobby meter **only for known, documented low-voltage DC** and continuity on **disconnected** circuits. Never measure resistance/continuity on a powered circuit.
2. Confirm probes: black **COM**, red **V/Ω** when measuring voltage. Do not use A/mA input for voltage testing.
3. Check charger label, output polarity and connector pinout **for the exact part**. Some barrel plugs have a separate centre ID/sense pin. Don't short any pins with the probes.
4. Do not open a laptop charger, desktop ATX PSU, or any other mains switch-mode device; components can retain hazardous energy even unplugged. Do not follow any portion of a tutorial probing mains AC, even if the video suggests it.
5. A nominal no-load output reading does **not** prove the PSU works under load. A starter/paperclip test is not a complete ATX functional test.
6. Laptop board repair videos are for observation until competence, schematics and appropriate instruments are established.
